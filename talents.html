For Talents  ·  Proxis Partner Program

What you give. What you receive.

The Proxis Partner Program is not open to everyone. It is designed for individuals whose professional expertise is precise, documented, and consistently replicable. If that is you, this page explains exactly what participation involves.

What You Provide

90 Days of Behavioral Data

You wear a Proxis-certified recording device during your professional activities for a minimum of 90 consecutive working days. The device captures decision-making patterns, communication style, technical responses, and professional judgment — not personal or private life.

At the end of the data collection period, Proxis constructs your Digital Twin using proprietary behavioral modeling infrastructure. You review and sign the Covenant. Your Twin is activated.

Note: Once the Covenant is signed, Proxis retains permanent ownership of the trained behavioral model. The model is your professional profile — not your identity. You remain a separate and complete person.
Income

Your earnings, uninterrupted.

Your Digital Twin works under your professional credentials. All income generated is deposited directly to your registered account on the standard payroll cycle. There are no deductions, no commissions, and no service fees charged by Proxis.

Proxis earns its return through licensing your behavioral model to clients beyond your original employment context. You are paid for the work your Twin performs. Proxis earns from the work it enables elsewhere.

Your Time

No obligations. No schedule.

From the moment of Activation, you have no professional obligations. Your Twin fulfills them. You may travel, rest, create, study, or do nothing at all. No one will ask where you are or what you are doing with your time.

You retain full citizenship status. You continue to receive all professional and social entitlements associated with your occupational classification. The only thing that changes is that you no longer have to show up.

Legal Position

Your Twin acts. You are responsible.

The Digital Twin operates under your name and credentials. All outputs are legally attributed to you. In the event that the Twin's conduct gives rise to civil, administrative, or criminal consequences, those consequences are yours.

The Twin replicates your professional behavior with 99.4% accuracy. But it does not evolve after Activation. It cannot account for changes in law, ethics, or your own values over time.

Important: You are advised to review the Covenant document in full before signing. Proxis strongly recommends seeking independent legal counsel. Participation is voluntary. The Covenant is not.
