Program Overview  ·  Proxis Partner Process

How the Program Works

The Proxis Partner Program operates in three phases. Each phase is designed to be precise, transparent, and permanent. There are no trial periods. There are no reversals.

Phase 01
Data Collection

We learn everything about how you work.

Proxis provides eligible partners with a certified behavioral recording device. The device is worn during professional activities — not at home, not in private life. It records how you think, decide, communicate, and respond under the conditions of your specific expertise.

The collection period lasts a minimum of 90 working days. During this time, you continue your professional life exactly as before. Nothing changes. You simply allow the process to observe.

Duration90 working days minimum
Data CapturedDecisions, communication patterns, technical judgment, response behavior
Data ExcludedPersonal life, private communications, non-professional activities
DeviceProxis PX-7 wearable — provided and retrieved by Proxis
Phase 02
Twin Generation

Your behavioral record becomes a certified model.

Following data collection, Proxis constructs your Digital Twin using proprietary deterministic behavioral simulation architecture. The Twin is not an approximation. It does not generate probable responses. It replicates the responses you would give, derived directly from observed data.

The generated model undergoes a certification review before Activation. You are provided with a summary of the Twin's behavioral parameters. You do not have access to the model itself — only to the outputs it is certified to produce.

ArchitectureDeterministic behavioral simulation (DBS-18)
Accuracy99.4% (±0.003, 95% confidence interval)
Post-activation learningNone — the model is fixed at Activation
Model ownershipTransferred permanently to Proxis upon Covenant signing
Notice: The Twin replicates you as you are at the time of data collection. It will not update to reflect who you become. All decisions made by the Twin after Activation are attributed to you in full.
Phase 03
Activation

You sign. Your Twin begins. You are free.

Once the Covenant is signed, your Digital Twin is activated immediately. It assumes your professional obligations, fulfills your civic duties, and generates income under your name and credentials. You receive that income in full. You are not required to do anything further.

The Covenant is permanent from the moment of signing. There is no trial period, no grace period, and no withdrawal process. Proxis does not offer exceptions. The Department of Digital Continuity does not administer reversals.

Notice: You are strongly advised to read the full Covenant document before signing. Proxis recommends independent legal counsel. The decision is voluntary. Its consequences are not.
